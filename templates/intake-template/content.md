## FAQ
<!-- becomes src/data/faqs.json -->

### Which organizations must comply with NERC CIP?
Registered entities on the bulk electric system — transmission and generator owners/operators, balancing authorities, and related registered functions. Obligations scale with the CIP-002 impact rating of your BES Cyber Systems.

### What is CIP-015 and when did it take effect?
CIP-015-1 requires Internal Network Security Monitoring inside the Electronic Security Perimeter. It became effective September 2, 2025, with an expanded EACMS/PACS revision in development.
