# CAN-SPAM compliance — outreach email (DRAFT)

Applies to any prospect/LP outreach email we send. Every commercial email must satisfy all of the
below (15 U.S.C. §7704). Fill the brackets before sending a single message.

## Required email footer (paste into every outreach template)
```
[LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME]
[PHYSICAL_MAILING_ADDRESS]   ← a real, monitored postal address (street, PO box, or CMRA)

You're receiving this because [REASON]. Not interested? Unsubscribe: [UNSUBSCRIBE_LINK]
```

## Compliance checklist
- [ ] **Accurate "From" / routing** — header info identifies [LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME], not deceptive.
- [ ] **Non-deceptive subject line** — reflects the actual content.
- [ ] **Identified as an ad** where applicable (relationship/solicitation context).
- [ ] **Valid physical postal address** in every message (the footer above).
- [ ] **Clear unsubscribe mechanism** — one-click or reply-to-unsubscribe, working for ≥30 days.
- [ ] **Honor opt-outs within 10 business days**; never sell/transfer an opted-out address.
- [ ] **Monitor on behalf of** — we're responsible even if a tool/agency sends for us.

> Note: the LP-sourcing outreach (Trivium) and any web-studio customer outreach both fall under
> CAN-SPAM. Keep the same monitored address and suppression list across both.
