- **Account & contact details.** Email address and any name/business details you provide.
- **Rebuild questionnaire answers.** The business facts, brand preferences, and assets you supply
  so we can rebuild your site.
- **Domain-ownership proof.** The DNS record, meta tag, or file you use to prove you control the
  domain before we do any rebuild work.

  Stripe customer/subscription identifier and plan status.
- **Usage & technical data.** Log data, IP address, and analytics needed to operate and secure the
  Service.
