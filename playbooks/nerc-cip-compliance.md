# Playbook: NERC-CIP Managed Compliance (critical-infrastructure B2B)

## Buyer & buying moment
Compliance managers, CISOs, and procurement teams at registered entities (utilities,
co-ops, IPPs, municipals). They arrive either pre-audit (anxious, deadline-driven) or
post-findings (urgent, budget unlocked). Decisions are committee-based and risk-averse;
the site's job is to survive scrutiny, not to excite.

## Sections that must exist
Archetype: b2b-compliance. Hero with explicit standards scope · regional-entity +
certification trust band · services mapped to CIP standard numbers · anonymized
engagement outcomes · team with former-auditor credentials · FAQ · contact with
response-time promise. Remove: anything playful, pricing pages (always scoped).

## Trust signals buyers scan for
Regional entities covered (SERC, WECC, RF, MRO, Texas RE, NPCC) · personnel certs
(CISSP, CISA, GICSP) · former regional-entity audit staff · explicit BCSI/CIP-011
information-handling statement · years across CMEP cycles · NDA-by-default posture.

## Schema (JSON-LD)
ProfessionalService (with areaServed per region, knowsAbout listing CIP standards),
Service per practice area, FAQPage. Person schema for principals with credentials.

## Tone & vocabulary
Voice: a former auditor explaining plainly — precise, calm, zero hype.
Use: standard numbers with versions (CIP-013-2), "registered entity", "evidence",
"audit-ready", "mitigation", "internal controls". Never use: "hacker-proof",
"guaranteed compliance", "100% secure", fear-mongering breach imagery, exclamation points.

## Conversion pattern
Primary CTA: fixed-scope gap assessment (low-commitment, defined deliverable).
Form: name, work email, organization, regional entity, message. Never ask for asset
details or anything resembling BCSI — and say so under the form.

## Legal & compliance must-haves
BCSI handling notice (footer + form) · privacy policy · accessibility statement
(procurement teams check) · no client names without written consent — sector + region
+ outcome only.

## Imagery direction
Transmission/substation photography, control-room aesthetics, documentation motifs.
Credible: monochrome industrial, schematic lines. Cheap: stock handshakes, padlock
clip-art, hooded-hacker imagery.

## FAQ topics that earn AI-search citations
Which organizations must comply with NERC CIP · what a regional entity audit involves ·
what CIP-015/INSM requires and its effective date · low-impact entity obligations ·
how BCSI must be protected · what happens after a potential noncompliance ·
CIP-013 supply-chain requirements.

## Never do
Never name clients or imply zero-violation guarantees. Never publish specifics that
map any entity's infrastructure. Never use fear-based selling — buyers are regulators-
adjacent and find it amateur. Never bury certifications below the fold.
