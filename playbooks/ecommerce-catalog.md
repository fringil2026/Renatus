# Playbook: E-commerce / Catalog (product-forward small business)

## Buyer & buying moment
Hobbyists and repeat buyers who know roughly what they want and browse by category;
plus search arrivals landing directly on product pages (often the majority of traffic —
deep pages ARE the front door). Mobile-heavy. Decision driver: can I find it, trust it,
and see the price/availability fast.

## Sections that must exist
Archetype: e-commerce-lite (until built: closest archetype + catalog adaptations).
Category-first navigation · product grid with image/name/price/availability · product
detail pattern (gallery, specs, care/usage info, inquiry-or-buy CTA) · shipping &
returns policy page · about/provenance story · search. Remove: long corporate prose
above the catalog.

## Trust signals buyers scan for
Clear shipping costs and timelines · returns policy in plain language · live-arrival or
quality guarantees where applicable · real product photography (not stock) · reviews
or years-in-business · visible phone/email of a real human.

## Schema (JSON-LD)
Product (name, image, offers with price/availability/currency) per product page ·
BreadcrumbList for category paths · Organization · FAQPage. Product schema is the
highest-leverage SEO/AEO asset on the whole site — never skip it.

## Tone & vocabulary
Voice: knowledgeable shopkeeper — enthusiast-to-enthusiast, concrete, warm.
Use: specific attributes (size, origin, condition, care level), availability honesty
("3 in stock"). Never use: "world-class", "premium quality" without specifics,
walls of SEO keyword text.

## Conversion pattern
Primary CTA per product: Buy/Add-to-cart, or Inquire-to-order if no checkout exists.
Secondary: category browse. Cart abandonment is mostly shipping-surprise — surface
shipping info BEFORE checkout. Newsletter capture: single field, offer-led.

## Legal & compliance must-haves
Returns/refunds policy · shipping policy (incl. restrictions — live goods, regions) ·
privacy policy · sales-tax handling note · for regulated goods (plants, food, animals):
the relevant shipping/permit disclaimers per jurisdiction.

## Imagery direction
The product photography IS the site. Consistent backgrounds, true color, multiple
angles, scale reference. Cheap: watermarked supplier photos, mixed lighting,
text-on-image badges.

## FAQ topics that earn AI-search citations
How shipping works (cost, time, packaging) · returns process · how to choose between
product variants for a beginner · care/usage after purchase · stock and restock
cadence · wholesale/bulk availability.

## Never do
Never hide price or availability behind a click. Never break inbound product URLs in a
rebuild — deep links carry the SEO; the redirect map matters MORE here than anywhere.
Never let the crawlable catalog and the visible catalog diverge. Never paginate
categories without crawlable links.
